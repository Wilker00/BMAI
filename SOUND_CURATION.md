# BMAI sound curation

The live library prioritizes material useful for music production: drum one-shots, basses, loops, percussion, pads, ambience, guitars, vinyl textures and vocal material.

Sonic Pi’s toy drums, chip blips, and synth bleeps are archived under `archive/excluded-sounds/video-game-and-novelty/sonic-pi` and are not in the live library. What remains from that pack is musical: Arovane beats, The Black Dog pads, ambience, guitar, vinyl, and breaks.

## Curated beat styles

- R&B: TR-909 kick, snare and hat, with a short 808 sub.
- House: processed studio kick and snare, a recorded hi-hat, and a sub.
- Trap: trap one-shots with a long sub-808.
- Drum & bass: distorted kick, LinnDrum snare, recorded hat, 808 sub.
- Acoustic: recorded Szpaderski kick, Pearl snare, and closed hat.

The beat-style selector changes both the four sample slots and the underlying 16-step groove. "Humanize selected beat" creates a controlled rhythmic variation while preserving the backbeat.
